How it works

From a paper on your desk to a mark for every student.

  1. Add the paper

    Take a photo of each page, choose photos you already took, or pick a PDF of up to 20 pages. Add the answer key at the end if the paper has one. No paper at hand? Describe the test and AI writes the questions.

  2. AI reads it

    It finds each question and its options, page by page, and works out the answers. A printed answer key always comes first.

  3. Check what it is unsure about

    Questions it is sure of are ready. The rest wait in a short list for you: two AI checks that disagree, a figure it cannot see, a line it could not read. One click settles each.

  4. Post it to your students

    Choose which students write it, a time limit, and when it opens and closes. Students find it in AnyTutor once it opens.

  5. Students write it on a phone or a computer

    One question at a time, and you can mix the questions up for each student. When a student hands in, their marks and the right answers appear at once.

  6. See who needs help

    Each student has a page with their average, their weakest chapters and the tests they missed. If an answer key was wrong, fix it once and everyone is marked again.

Questions

Before you start.

What do I need to use it?

A browser, on a computer, a tablet or a phone. Open AnyTutor and log in: there is nothing to install. On a computer your tuition sits beside a sidebar; on a phone it is laid out for the small screen. Your students use it the same way.

Do my students need an email or a phone number?

No. Students make their own account with a username and a password, and AnyTutor keeps no email address or phone number. Give them your tuition's join code, or a group's or course's link or QR code; they ask to join, and you choose whether to let them in.

Is the AI always right?

No, so you stay in charge. A printed answer key comes first; otherwise two AI models answer each question and a third settles a tie. Anything still unsure waits for you to check before the test goes out. If an answer turns out wrong later, fix it on the test's page and every student is marked again.

What does the AI in a plan pay for?

Three things: AI reading the pages of a question paper (₹2.40 a page), AI writing questions when you describe a test (₹0.60 a question) and AI writing worked solutions (₹0.40 each). Free comes with ₹20 of AI to start; Basic and Pro come with AI every month. Everything else works on every plan.

What happens when the month's AI runs out?

AI work waits until your plan's next month, or until you move to a bigger plan. Tests, students and results keep working as usual.

What if my tuition outgrows its plan?

Everyone already in stays in. Letting more students or tutors in waits until you move to a bigger plan.

How do I pay?

In AnyTutor, open Plan (on a phone it is in Settings). Choose a plan and how many months, pay by UPI to the UPI id it shows with the code it gives you in the payment note, then click I have paid. The plan starts once the payment has been checked.

Can other tutors at my tuition use it too?

Yes. Add the other tutors from Settings, and each gets a login of their own.

Can I delete my tuition?

Yes. The owner can delete the tuition, and everything in it, from Settings, once any payment for a plan has been checked. See the privacy policy for what is kept and what is deleted.
