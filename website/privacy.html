AnyTutor is an app for tuition teachers and their students, in a browser and on Android. This page says what the app keeps, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. The app is run by FILL-IN the business's legal name and address.

What the app keeps

  • About tutors: their name, their username, their password (kept only as a salted hash, never as the password itself) and the name of their tuition.
  • About students: their name, their username, their password (kept only as a salted hash, never as the password itself), their class, and the subjects, groups and courses their tutors let them into.
  • Signing in with Google: for anyone who chooses it, the number Google gives their Google account, and the name on it. Never the email address.
  • Invitations: the code in each group's and course's link, and a student's request to join, until a tutor lets them in or declines.
  • Tests and results: the pictures of question papers that tutors add, the questions, each student's answers, their marks and how long they took.
  • The Library: the notes and files tutors add for their students.
  • Plans and AI credit: each tuition's plan and the days it runs, its AI credit and what it was spent on, and its payments for plans: the plan, the months, the amount, the code written in the payment note and the payment reference noted when the payment is checked.
  • Attendance: each class's registers: the day, who took the register, and which students were there.
  • Timetables: when each class meets each week (the days, the start time and how long it runs), the link a tutor adds for joining a class online, and a short note for students.
  • Fees, for tuitions that use them: each student's monthly fee and the payments noted against it (the amount, the month, how and when it was paid, any note such as a UPI reference, and the receipt number), a student's message that they have paid, and the UPI id and name the tuition's owner adds so that students can pay them. Only the tuition's owner sees fees.
  • About phones: a random id made when the app is installed, the phone's model, its Android version, the app's version, and when each account last used the app on it.
  • Sign-in records: when someone signed in, with which username, whether it worked, and the install id. They are deleted after 90 days.
  • Running the service: daily counts of how much each tuition uses the app, and records of server errors, which are deleted after 30 days.

The app does not ask anyone for an email address or a phone number, and the app's own records hold no IP addresses. A UPI id that a tutor adds for fees can be made from their phone number; the app tells them that students and parents will see it.

Who else handles it

  • Neon runs the app's database, its server and its file storage, in FILL-IN the region.
  • Google (the Gemini API) and Groq receive the pages of question papers and the questions, so that AI can read the papers, work out the answers and write questions. FILL-IN: say whether Google's unpaid Gemini tier is used. On that tier Google may use what is sent to it to improve its products, and people may read it; on a paid tier it does not.
  • Google Sign-In, for those who choose to sign in with Google: Google confirms the account to the app, and so knows that its user signed in to AnyTutor.
  • GitHub hosts the Android app's downloads and the file it checks for updates. GitHub sees the IP address of anyone who downloads.
  • The web app, the same app in a browser, is served by Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages), with this website, and loads some of its parts from other sites: letters for Hindi, Tamil and other scripts from Google's font server, Google's sign-in library (which the app loads although sign-in with Google is not offered in the browser yet), and the PDF reader for question papers from jsDelivr. Each of them sees the IP address of the browser, as with any website.
  • Payments are made from people's own UPI apps: a tutor's for a plan, and a student's or parent's for fees, straight to the tutor. The app never sees bank or card details and never holds the money.
  • Online classes run on the service a tutor chooses, such as Google Meet, Zoom or Jitsi, not in AnyTutor. Tapping Join opens the tutor's link in that service's app or in the browser. The app sends that service nothing about the student, though the browser may tell it that the link was opened from AnyTutor. The class itself is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

This website uses no cookies, no tracking and no scripts.

Deleting data

  • A whole tuition: its owner can delete it from Settings in the app, with their password, once any payment for a plan has been checked. That deletes its tests, questions, question papers, results, attendance, fee records and Library files, and the accounts of its tutors who are in no other tuition. Students keep their own accounts: they lose that tuition and what they wrote there, and keep everything at any other tuition.
  • What is kept: records of payments for plans and AI credit (the plan, the amount, the dates, the reference and the tuition's name), for the accounts. FILL-IN how long they are kept.
  • A student's account: a student can delete it in the app, from their profile (Delete account), with their password, or by choosing their Google account again if they sign in with Google. That deletes the account and everything of theirs at every tuition: their answers, marks, attendance and requests to join. The fee payments a tutor noted for them stay in that tuition's records under the student's name, as the tutor's accounts, until the tuition itself is deleted.
