Tuition Tests is an Android app for tuition teachers and their students. This page says what the app keeps, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. The app is run by FILL-IN the business's legal name and address.

What the app keeps

  • About tutors: their name, their username, their password (kept only as a salted hash, never as the password itself) and the name of their tuition.
  • About students: their name, their username, their password (kept only as a salted hash, never as the password itself), their class, and the subjects, groups and courses their tutors let them into.
  • Signing in with Google: for anyone who chooses it, the number Google gives their Google account, and the name on it. Never the email address.
  • Invitations: the code in each group's and course's link, and a student's request to join, until a tutor lets them in or declines.
  • Tests and results: the pictures of question papers that tutors add, the questions, each student's answers, their marks and how long they took.
  • The Library: the notes and files tutors add for their students.
  • AI credit: each tuition's credit, what it was spent on, and its payment requests: the amount, the code written in the payment note and the payment reference noted when the payment is checked.
  • About phones: a random id made when the app is installed, the phone's model, its Android version, the app's version, and when each account last used the app on it.
  • Sign-in records: when someone signed in, with which username, whether it worked, and the install id. They are deleted after 90 days.
  • Running the service: daily counts of how much each tuition uses the app, and records of server errors, which are deleted after 30 days.

The app does not ask anyone for an email address or a phone number, and the app's own records hold no IP addresses.

  • Google Sign-In, for those who choose to sign in with Google: Google confirms the account to the app, and so knows that its user signed in to Tuition Tests.
  • GitHub hosts this website, the app's downloads and the file the app checks for updates. GitHub sees the IP address of anyone who visits or downloads.
  • Payments are made from the tutor's own UPI app. The app never sees bank or card details.

Deleting data

  • A whole tuition: its owner can delete it from Settings in the app, with their password. That deletes its tests, questions, question papers, results and Library files, and the accounts of its tutors who are in no other tuition. Students keep their own accounts: they lose that tuition and what they wrote there, and keep everything at any other tuition.
  • What is kept: records of payments (the amount, the date, the reference and the tuition's name), for the accounts. FILL-IN how long they are kept.
  • A student's account: a student can delete it in the app, from their profile (Delete account), with their password, or by choosing their Google account again if they sign in with Google. That deletes the account and everything of theirs at every tuition: their answers, marks and requests to join.
