Using the app

Tutors sign up in the app and make a tuition. They add their students, who log in with a username and a PIN, and they can add other tutors. Students who already have an account can ask to join a tuition with its code, and a tutor decides whether to let them in.

AI credit and payments

  • AI reading question papers and AI writing questions are paid for from prepaid AI credit. Everything else in the app is free.
  • The app shows the price before every AI job, and charges only for work the AI finishes.
  • Credit is bought by UPI from the app and added once the payment has been checked.
  • Prices can change. The app always shows the current price.
  • Deleting a tuition also ends its unused credit. The app says how much is left before the owner confirms.

AI can make mistakes

AI reads question papers and works out answers, and it can get them wrong. The app shows tutors the questions it is unsure of, and nothing reaches students until a tutor posts the test. A wrong answer can be fixed after a test, and every student is then marked again.
