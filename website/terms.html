Using the app

Tutors sign up in the app and make a tuition, and they can add other tutors. Students make their own accounts, with a username and a password or with Google, and ask to join a group or course with the link or QR code its tutor shares, or with the tuition's code. A tutor decides whether to let them in.

Plans and payments

  • Every tuition is on a plan: Free, Basic or Pro. A plan sets how many students and tutors the tuition can have and how much AI work comes with it.
  • AI reading question papers, writing questions and writing worked solutions is paid for from the AI that comes with the plan. Free gives an amount to start with; Basic and Pro give an amount every month, which does not carry over to the next month.
  • The app shows the price before every AI job, and charges only for work the AI finishes.
  • Plans are bought by UPI from the app, for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months, and start once the payment has been checked. Moving to a bigger plan starts it that day; days left on the smaller plan are not carried over.
  • When a plan ends, the tuition is on Free again. Nobody is turned off and nothing is deleted, but letting in more students or tutors than Free allows waits for a new plan.
  • Prices can change. The app always shows the current price.
  • Deleting a tuition also ends its plan and its unused AI. The app says what is left before the owner confirms, and a tuition with a plan payment still waiting to be checked can be deleted once it has been checked.

AI can make mistakes

AI reads question papers and works out answers, and it can get them wrong. The app shows tutors the questions it is unsure of, and nothing reaches students until a tutor posts the test. A wrong answer can be fixed after a test, and every student is then marked again.
